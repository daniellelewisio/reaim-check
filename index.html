The Re-Aim Check

When you are running on empty, the instinct is to rest, or to burn the whole business down. Sometimes one of those is right. Often neither is. This check works out which kind of empty you are actually in, so you fix the real thing instead of the obvious one. Six honest questions. Two minutes.

One rule: answer for how things have actually felt this past month, not how you wish they felt, and not how they looked on your best day.
